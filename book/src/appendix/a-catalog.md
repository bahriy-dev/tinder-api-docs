## Not listed

These kinds of results from a "tinder api" search were left out on purpose:

- Tools that create fake or bulk accounts or get around phone verification
- Tools that unlock paid features for free or expose other people's data
- Selenium/Appium swipe bots with no research value
- "Tinder-like" app clones and unrelated namesakes (such as the old 37signals Campfire library `collectiveidea/tinder`)
